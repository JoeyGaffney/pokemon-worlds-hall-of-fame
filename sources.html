The Pokemon TCG Worlds Hall of Fame is compiled from historical Pokémon World Championship results, archived event websites, tournament records, and other historical sources.

Some older World Championships have incomplete surviving records. Where an exact placement could not be verified, the database preserves only the level of detail supported by the available source.
